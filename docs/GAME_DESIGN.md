# Game Design

Motive is designed to make the classic party game of Mafia fast, fair, and fun to play online with friends and colleagues.

## Design Goals

1. **Simplicity First**: Clear screens, clean controls, and zero clutter. Anyone can pick it up and play immediately.
2. **Server Truth**: Secrets remain secret. Players cannot cheat by inspecting network traffic or web elements.
3. **Pacing & Tension**: Timed phases keep matches moving briskly without long pauses.
4. **Accessible Everywhere**: Runs smoothly in modern web browsers on desktop, laptop, or mobile screens.
5. **Flexible Party Play**: Works for casual friends or company team-building events with 4 to 12 players.
