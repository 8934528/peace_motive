# Game Rules

Motive is a party game of mystery, deduction, and deception.

## Players & Setup

- **Players**: 4 to 12 players.
- **Teams**:
  - **Town**: Uninformed majority. They must find and vote out all Mafia members.
  - **Mafia**: Informed minority. They secretly know who their teammates are and eliminate Town members each night.

## How to Play

1. **Secret Roles**: When the game begins, every player is given a secret card: Mafia, Doctor, Detective, or Villager.
2. **Night Phase**:
   - The Mafia votes to eliminate a player.
   - The Doctor chooses one player to protect from death.
   - The Detective investigates one player to discover if they are Mafia.
3. **Morning Report**: The town wakes up to see who survived or died during the night.
4. **Day Discussion**: Players debate and deduce who they think is lying.
5. **Day Voting**:
   - Living players vote for one suspect to eliminate, or vote for `Nobody`.
   - Players cannot vote for themselves.
   - Eliminated players become spectators and can no longer vote or act.

## Winning the Game

- **Town Wins**: When all Mafia members have been eliminated.
- **Mafia Wins**: When the number of living Mafia players equals or exceeds the number of living Town players.
